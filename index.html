Animal Arrows

Slide the arrows out to free the hidden animals!

How to play: every arrow slides in the direction its head points. Tap an arrow — if nothing is in its way it zooms off the board. If another arrow blocks it, it bumps back and you lose a heart (3 per level). Clear every arrow to reveal the animal! Stuck? Tap Hint. Big animal? Pinch or use + / − to zoom, then drag to look around.
